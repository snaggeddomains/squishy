FAQ

Questions, answered

What's actually in a box?

Each month you get 3–4 premium squishy and sensory toys plus a surprise insert (a collectible, sticker, or rare "golden squish"). Boxes are loosely themed and curated for ages 4+.

How much does it cost?

$35/month month-to-month. Prepay to save: 10% off for 3 months and 20% off for 6 months. Local pickup saves an extra $5/month.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes — month-to-month plans can be paused or cancelled anytime. Prepaid plans run for the term you choose and then renew monthly.

Do you ship, or is it local pickup?

Both! We ship nationwide, and South Orange & Maplewood families can choose free local pickup at scheduled SOMA pickup events to save $5/month.

Are the toys safe?

We curate from established, parent-trusted brands and only include sensory-safe items. Boxes are recommended for ages 4+.

Who's behind SomaSquish?

Two third graders from Marshall School in South Orange — Ryan Schutz and Ethan Greenberg — united by squishy toys and the Baltimore Orioles. (With a little grown-up help on the shipping.)
